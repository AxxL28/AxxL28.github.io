MULTIPLAYER ROGUELIKE • GAMEPLAY PROGRAMMING

Blood & Lineage

A 3D roguelike-Musou hybrid built around fast-paced combat, unique character abilities, multiplayer gameplay, and procedurally generated levels.

ENGINE Unreal Engine
LANGUAGE C++
ROLE Gameplay Programmer & Team Lead

01 • OVERVIEW

Building the gameplay foundation

Blood & Lineage was developed as a multiplayer-focused capstone project. The goal was to create a fast, replayable action experience while keeping the underlying gameplay systems modular enough to support multiple playable classes and a large collection of abilities.

My work focused heavily on gameplay architecture. I designed the core modular gameplay ability system, implemented 13 of the project's 16 unique abilities, and worked on the network replication required to keep abilities, animations, effects, and attacks synchronized in multiplayer.

04 • CHALLENGES

Problems worth solving

01

Keeping systems modular

Multiple playable classes needed different gameplay identities without creating a separate architecture for every character. The solution was to build shared systems that could be extended with class-specific behaviour.

02

Maintaining multiplayer consistency

Gameplay could not rely solely on local execution. Abilities, attacks, animations, and effects needed to remain consistent between players and the server.

03

Managing a team project

As team lead, I also helped coordinate development, organize tasks, troubleshoot issues, and keep the project moving toward a stable playable build.
